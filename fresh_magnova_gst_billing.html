📖 How To Use Guide

Step-by-step guide with examples for all entries.

🧾 Sale Invoice

1. Click Sale Entry → fill Invoice No., Date, Buyer, GSTIN.
2. Add item rows: Description, HSN, Qty, Rate, GST%.
3. Click 💾 Save Sale → 📂 Save to History → 🖨️ Print.
EXAMPLE: Inv#383 · Buyer: M/s JAMS INDUSTRIES · Item: LDPE SHRINK FILM 520MM · Qty:180 · Rate:₹135.60 · GST:5% · Total: ₹28,801.44

⚡ Smart Parser

Format: [Qty] [Item] at Rs [Rate] — road transport, vehicle [VehicleNo]
EXAMPLE:
Invoice for M/s JAMS INDUSTRIES Varanasi — 180 LDPE Shrink Film at Rs 135.60 each, road transport, vehicle UP65AB1234

📦 Purchase Entry

Enter Supplier Name, Bill No., Date, GSTIN → Add items → Save Purchase (stock updates automatically).
